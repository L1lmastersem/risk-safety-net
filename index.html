Baseline equity With safety net

How the safety net works: Layer 1 shrinks each new trade as the day's combined loss grows, so its worst case can't push the day past the limit. Layer 2 caps any single trade at a fixed share of the daily limit, which catches one oversized first trade of the day.

How risk per trade is estimated: a trade export has each trade's outcome but not its original stop distance. The tool uses a losing trade's loss directly and half a winning trade's gain as a rough stand-in. Treat results as directionally correct, not exact to the dollar.

Sample data: two made-up symbols over one year of trading days, generated in the browser. It is not real trading data.
